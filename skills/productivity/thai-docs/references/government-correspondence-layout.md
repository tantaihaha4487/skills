# Thai government correspondence: external letters and shared body rules

Source: [OBEC, แนวปฏิบัติเกี่ยวกับการพิมพ์หนังสือราชการตามระเบียบงานสารบรรณ](https://personnel.obec.go.th/web/แนวปฏิบัติงานสารบัญ), especially §§1–3. The page links example DOCX files for [external letters](https://personnel.obec.go.th/home/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/ตัวอย่างหนังสือภายนอก.docx), [internal memoranda](https://personnel.obec.go.th/home/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/ตัวอย่างหนังสือภายใน.docx), and [stamped letters](https://personnel.obec.go.th/home/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/ตัวอย่างหนังสือประทับตรา.docx). Inspect an applicable example or institutional template when available. The examples contain placeholder text and some mixed `TH SarabunPSK`/`TH SarabunIT๙` runs; do not copy the font mixing into a new document.

This reference gives source-backed formatting for `หนังสือภายนอก` and the body-paragraph treatment that OBEC §3.2.3 also assigns to an internal `บันทึกข้อความ`. For the internal form's distinct header and signature treatment, also read [internal-government-memo-layout.md](internal-government-memo-layout.md). Do not apply the external-letter closing, emblem size, or field order to an internal memorandum, essay, or report. Follow explicit user requirements and a supplied applicable form first. The web page is guidance, not proof of universal or up-to-date legal compliance.

## Page and emblem

- Use 3 cm left and 2 cm right margins and single (`1.0`) line spacing within ordinary paragraphs. On A4, the usable text width is 16 cm; the page describes a 0–16 cm printing ruler. Control gaps between paragraphs separately. (OBEC §§1.1–1.3)
- For a letter on Garuda letterhead (`กระดาษตราครุฑ`, form `แบบที่ ๒๘`), set the Garuda emblem height to 3 cm and place it about 1.5 cm below the top edge. Preserve its aspect ratio; the page does not specify a width. An internal-memo emblem is 1.5 cm high and has its own profile. Use an authorized form/emblem asset rather than inventing one. (intro, §§2.1–2.2)

## External-letter flow

- Keep the applicable form's `ที่`, sender/address, date, `เรื่อง`, salutation, `อ้างถึง` and `สิ่งที่ส่งมาด้วย` when present, body, closing, signatory name/position, and owner-office/contact area in their proper order. The OBEC example illustrates this flow; omit optional fields when not applicable rather than printing fictitious values. (§3.1 and linked external-letter DOCX)
- Separate `เรื่อง`, salutation, `อ้างถึง`, and `สิ่งที่ส่งมาด้วย` from the preceding item by one normal line plus 6 pt before the next item (`1 Enter + before 6 pt`). Apply the same gap between the reason, request, and conclusion parts of the body. Keep the text within each paragraph single-spaced. (§§3.1.1–3.1.2)
- Use a 2.5 cm first-line indent for the reason, request, and conclusion body paragraphs. This is a paragraph indent, not an extra page margin. (§3.1.3)
- Put the closing one normal line after the last conclusion line plus 12 pt before the closing (`1 Enter + before 12 pt`). Leave the equivalent of three blank typing lines before the full signatory name, and three blank typing lines between the signatory position and the owner-office identification. Reproduce these gaps with paragraph spacing/positioning where practical; do not build layout from long chains of spaces or empty paragraphs. (§§3.1.4–3.1.6)
- The number of lines per page is content-dependent; the page allows reasonable adjustment to maintain the form and appearance. Other official-letter types are treated by analogy, not by blindly cloning this field sequence. (§§3.3–3.4 and note)

## Unspecified values and verification

The page does not state universal top/bottom margins, body font size, or a numeric width for the 3 cm external-letter emblem. Linked DOCX examples supply examples, not universal mandates. Use a current applicable institutional form where supplied, otherwise the skill's general defaults for unset values. Keep the user's selected font family throughout; the linked examples' mixed families do not override that requirement.

Inspect the final DOCX and rendered PDF for page geometry, emblem placement, field order, single-spaced paragraphs, 2.5 cm body first-line indents, 6/12 pt inter-item gaps, signature area, Thai glyphs, and consistent font family. Distinguish a checked visual match from merely matching DOCX settings.
