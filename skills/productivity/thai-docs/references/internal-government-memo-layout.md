# Thai internal government memorandum: `บันทึกข้อความ` (`แบบที่ ๒๙`)

Source: [การพิมพ์หนังสือราชการภาษาไทย — การจัดทำกระดาษบันทึกข้อความ](https://ssj01.phoubon.in.th/doc/rab/a9.pdf), 6-page PDF, especially pp. 1–5. The source describes a specific internal-memorandum form and includes an example on p. 6. It is not evidence that these measurements govern essays, reports, every official letter, or every institution's current form. Follow explicit user requirements and an applicable supplied form first. Do not claim legal or institution-wide compliance from this PDF alone.

## Source-backed memo layout

- Use the `บันทึกข้อความ` form (`แบบที่ ๒๙`), preserving its field order and labels. Put dotted fill-in lines after `ส่วนราชการ`, `ที่`, `วันที่`, and `เรื่อง`. Do not add a thick solid dividing line between the header and the message body. (p. 1)
- Set the left margin to 3 cm and the right margin to 2 cm. The source describes the printing ruler as spanning 0–16 cm, consistent with the available width of an A4 page after those side margins. This is the text area, not a 16 cm paragraph indent. (pp. 2–3)
- Use single (`1.0`) line spacing for ordinary text within paragraphs. The `บันทึกข้อความ` title has its own explicit line-height exception below. Keep paragraph gaps separate from within-paragraph line spacing. (p. 2)
- Size the Garuda emblem to 1.5 × 1.5 cm without distorting its proportions. Use an authorized emblem asset supplied with the form or by the user; do not invent or download an unverified replacement. (p. 3)
- Format `บันทึกข้อความ` in bold at 29 pt with exact (`Exactly`) 35 pt line spacing. Format the explicitly named header labels `ส่วนราชการ`, `วันที่`, and `เรื่อง` in bold at 20 pt. The guide does **not** specify a size for `ที่`; match the supplied form or the surrounding header consistently rather than claiming a source-mandated value. (p. 4)
- Place the salutation/opening line one normal line after `เรื่อง`, with an additional 6 pt of space before it (`1 Enter + 6 pt` in the guide). Reproduce the visual gap with paragraph spacing/positioning where practical, not a chain of empty paragraphs. (p. 5)
- For the reasons and request portions of the body, the PDF refers to the practice for an external official letter but does not restate its measurements. Do not infer body font size, first-line indent, or section spacing from that reference. (p. 5)
- Leave the equivalent of three blank typing lines between the concluding text and the full name of the document owner (`4 Enter` in the guide). Keep the signature area with its name and position rather than treating it as a report footer. (p. 5)
- Choose the number of text lines on each page to suit the amount of content and visual balance. The source explicitly permits departments to adjust formatting where needed while preserving the form and its appearance. (pp. 5–6)

## Values this guide does not settle

The prose does not prescribe top/bottom margins, body font family or size, or a font size for `ที่`. Figure 1.1 shows one Word page-setup example (top 1.9 cm, bottom 2.54 cm); treat those as example settings, not a universal mandate. Use an applicable supplied template for unsettled values, otherwise the skill's general defaults. The user's single-family font rule still applies: use the selected `TH SarabunPSK` or `TH SarabunIT๙` throughout, and ask if the choice conflicts with a supplied form. Do not mix those families.

The report-specific 18 pt centered chapter headings, new-page chapters, numbered-only lists, centered table cells, and table-of-contents layout do not automatically apply to this internal-memorandum form.

## Verification for a generated or restyled memo

Inspect the DOCX structure and its final render for the asymmetric side margins and 16 cm text width, emblem dimensions, title and header-label sizes, dotted fields, absence of a header divider, single-spaced body, salutation gap, and signature gap. Check all pages, especially a continuation page. If a source image or template was unavailable, state the precise limitation instead of asserting a perfect form match.
