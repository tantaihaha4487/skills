# Thai internal government memorandum: `บันทึกข้อความ` (`แบบที่ ๒๙`)

Sources: [การพิมพ์หนังสือราชการภาษาไทย — การจัดทำกระดาษบันทึกข้อความ](https://ssj01.phoubon.in.th/doc/rab/a9.pdf), 6-page PDF, especially pp. 1–5, and [OBEC's government-correspondence guidance](https://personnel.obec.go.th/web/แนวปฏิบัติงานสารบัญ), §3.2. The PDF describes a specific internal-memorandum form and includes an example on p. 6. OBEC's page supplies external-letter body rules that §3.2.3 explicitly applies to internal memoranda. These sources do not prove that every institution uses the same current form. Follow explicit user requirements and an applicable supplied form first; do not claim legal or institution-wide compliance from them alone.

## Source-backed memo layout

- Use the `บันทึกข้อความ` form (`แบบที่ ๒๙`), preserving its field order and labels. Put dotted fill-in lines after `ส่วนราชการ`, `ที่`, `วันที่`, and `เรื่อง`. Do not add a thick solid dividing line between the header and the message body. The PDF example and OBEC's linked internal-memo sample place the Garuda at upper left and center the `บันทึกข้อความ` title across the header area; verify that visual relationship rather than leaving the title left-aligned. (PDF pp. 1, 6; OBEC sample)
- Set the left margin to 3 cm and the right margin to 2 cm. The source describes the printing ruler as spanning 0–16 cm, consistent with the available width of an A4 page after those side margins. This is the text area, not a 16 cm paragraph indent. (pp. 2–3)
- Use single (`1.0`) line spacing for ordinary text within paragraphs. The `บันทึกข้อความ` title has its own explicit line-height exception below. Keep paragraph gaps separate from within-paragraph line spacing. (p. 2)
- The PDF specifies a 1.5 × 1.5 cm Garuda area. Fit a non-square emblem within that box without stretching it, and record the resulting dimensions. Use an asset supplied with the form or extracted from the [OBEC internal-memo example](https://personnel.obec.go.th/home/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/ตัวอย่างหนังสือภายใน.docx) after checking that file's origin and suitability; do not invent or download an unverified replacement. (p. 3)
- Format `บันทึกข้อความ` in bold at 29 pt with exact (`Exactly`) 35 pt line spacing. Format the explicitly named header labels `ส่วนราชการ`, `วันที่`, and `เรื่อง` in bold at 20 pt. The guide does **not** specify a size for `ที่`; match the supplied form or the surrounding header consistently rather than claiming a source-mandated value. (p. 4)
- Put the salutation/opening line in the next paragraph after `เรื่อง`, with 6 pt space before it (`1 Enter + before 6 pt` in the guide). The Enter is the paragraph break, not an extra blank line or full line-height added to 6 pt. (p. 5)
- For the reasons, request, and conclusion portions of the body, follow the external-letter treatment in [government-correspondence-layout.md](government-correspondence-layout.md): 2.5 cm first-line indent and 6 pt before each part after a paragraph break. OBEC §3.2.3 explicitly applies its external-letter body and indentation rules to internal memoranda. The earlier PDF refers to external-letter practice but does not itself restate these measurements. Neither source mandates a body font size. (PDF p. 5; OBEC §§3.1.2–3.1.3, 3.2.3)
- Leave the equivalent of three blank typing lines between the concluding text and the full name of the document owner (`4 Enter` in the guide). Keep the signature area with its name and position rather than treating it as a report footer. (p. 5)
- Choose the number of text lines on each page to suit the amount of content and visual balance. The source explicitly permits departments to adjust formatting where needed while preserving the form and its appearance. (pp. 5–6)

## Values this guide does not settle

The prose does not prescribe top/bottom margins, body font family or size, or a font size for `ที่`. Figure 1.1 shows one Word page-setup example (top 1.9 cm, bottom 2.54 cm); treat those as example settings, not a universal mandate. Use an applicable supplied template for unsettled values, otherwise the skill's general defaults. The user's single-family font rule still applies: use the selected `TH SarabunPSK` or `TH SarabunIT๙` throughout, and ask if the choice conflicts with a supplied form. Do not mix those families, even when a downloaded sample mixes them.

The report-specific 18 pt centered chapter headings, new-page chapters, numbered-only lists, centered table cells, and table-of-contents layout do not automatically apply to this internal-memorandum form.

## Verification for a generated or restyled memo

Inspect the DOCX structure and its final render for the asymmetric side margins and 16 cm text width, emblem dimensions, title and header-label sizes, dotted fields, absence of a header divider, single-spaced body, 2.5 cm body first-line indents, salutation/part gaps, and signature gap. Check all pages, especially a continuation page. If a source image or template was unavailable, state the precise limitation instead of asserting a perfect form match.
